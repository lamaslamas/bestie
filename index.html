Hi, I’m Andrea.

I photograph wild animals in Salento, in the south of Italy, and wherever else I can sit still long enough. Most of it is handheld, in manual, waiting for the light and the animal to agree. Some photos ask for colour, some for black and white.

By day I work in design and video — that’s on my other site.
